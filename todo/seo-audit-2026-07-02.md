## Executive Summary

The technical and structured-data foundation is now genuinely strong and largely
"done." Since the June 25 audit the site added Google Tag Manager (analytics is
now live), the legal pages resolve correctly at clean URLs with proper redirects,
security headers and long-cache asset headers verify live, the schema `@graph` is
well-connected, and `llms.txt` is serving. Nothing on the site is blocking
indexing or causing a penalty.

The score barely moved because the ceiling on this site is no longer technical —
it is **off-site authority and on-page trust**. Three things cap ranking for
"website redesign Worcester MA" and similar queries: (1) `sameAs` is still empty
because Google Business Profile isn't public yet, so the entity has no external
corroboration; (2) there are still zero third-party reviews or client
testimonials; (3) all topical weight sits on a single URL with no dedicated
service or location page. None of those are fixable with a code change — they need
GBP to go live, a first real review, and one new page.

Two **new, concrete** issues surfaced this pass that the prior audit did not flag,
both cheap to fix: the two `noindex` legal pages are listed in `sitemap.xml`
(mixed signal to Google), and the `<title>` still carries no geo keyword, which is
a real miss for local-pack eligibility.

## Top Critical / High Issues

1. **`sameAs` empty — no external entity corroboration** *(High, blocked on GBP).*
   Biggest single ceiling on both classic ranking and AI-search trust. Populate
   the moment GBP is public; add LinkedIn or any second real profile alongside.
2. **Zero third-party reviews / testimonials** *(High).* For a 2026-founded studio
   asking for a lead-form submit, this is the top conversion and trust gap. One
   real Quán Phở client quote is the highest-leverage single addition.
3. **All weight on one URL** *(High).* No standalone service or location page.
   `/website-redesign-worcester-ma` would expand topical + geo surface immediately.
4. **`noindex` legal pages listed in `sitemap.xml`** *(Medium — NEW).* `/privacy-policy`
   and `/terms` are `noindex, follow` yet appear in the sitemap. That's a
   contradictory signal — a sitemap says "index this." Remove both entries;
   keep only the homepage. Clean 10-second fix.
5. **Title tag has no geo keyword** *(Medium — NEW).* Current:
   `Website Redesign for Service Businesses | TechPersona Studio`. Local pack and
   local organic weight the title heavily. Add the city, e.g.
   `Website Redesign in Worcester, MA | TechPersona Studio`.

## Top Quick Wins

1. Remove `/privacy-policy` and `/terms` from `sitemap.xml` (2 min).
2. Add "Worcester, MA" to the `<title>` (2 min).
3. Refresh homepage `<lastmod>` in sitemap to today (content changed since 06-25;
   sitemap still says `2026-06-25`) (1 min).
4. Add a soft pricing anchor ("projects start at …") to the audit or FAQ section (15 min).
5. Populate `sameAs` the day GBP goes public (5 min when unblocked).

## Performance & Core Web Vitals — 74/100 (−2)

**New variable:** Google Tag Manager is now loaded in `<head>` (async). The prior
audit stated "no third-party scripts detected" — that's no longer true. GTM is the
one third-party on the critical path now; it's async and CSP-scoped, so impact is
modest, but it's the most likely source of any INP/main-thread cost going forward.

- **LCP (Good):** hero portrait preloaded with `fetchpriority="high"`, WebP served.
- **CLS (monitor):** three `<video>` elements have posters but **no explicit
  `width`/`height` attributes** — relies on CSS for aspect ratio. Verify the CSS
  reserves space; otherwise posters can still shift. Legal to add intrinsic dims.
- **INP (Good, watch):** minimal first-party JS (`index.js` ~741 lines). Watch GTM
  and the VI/EN toggle.
- **Media weight (Medium):** three autoplay/loop videos total **~15.5 MB**
  (6.4 + 4.6 + 4.4 MB), `preload="metadata"`. They autoplay site-wide including
  mobile. Consider `preload="none"` + play-on-intersection for the two below-fold
  sample videos, or a WebM source, to cut mobile data and decode cost.

| Finding | Severity | Effort |
|---------|----------|--------|
| Videos lack intrinsic width/height | Medium | 15 min |
| ~15.5 MB of autoplay video, mobile | Medium | 1–2 hr |
| GTM now on critical path | Low | monitor in field data |
| No WebM video source | Low | 1 hr |
